07 / FAQ

Questions? Let's clear them up.

A few quick answers to questions clients often have before starting a project.

How do I start a project?

Simply send a message through WhatsApp or email and tell me what you need, what the project is for, and any important deadline or requirements.

Do you work with individuals or businesses?

Both. I work with businesses, organizations, entrepreneurs, creators and individuals who need professional creative content.

What information do you need before starting?

Usually, I need a clear description of what you want, your preferred style or examples, your brand information or materials, and your intended use for the final content.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. The project scope and agreed revision rounds are discussed before work begins so expectations are clear from the start.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the type and complexity of the project. Once I understand what you need, I can give you a realistic delivery timeframe.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. Projects can be handled remotely, so you can work with AU Creative Studios regardless of your location.

What types of websites do you create?

I create business websites, landing pages, portfolio websites, personal brand websites and service-based websites. For advanced features such as e-commerce, booking or payment systems, contact me first so I can confirm the project requirements.

Can you help with hosting and deployment?

Yes. I can help with basic deployment and hosting setup once the project is complete, depending on the platform and requirements.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on the service, scope, complexity and turnaround time. Contact me with your project details and I'll provide the appropriate quote.
