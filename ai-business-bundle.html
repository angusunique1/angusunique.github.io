WHY I CREATED IT

This Book Started With a Problem I Had Myself.

I didn't create this because AI was a trend. I created it because I wanted to understand how to use it better.

When I started using AI for business and work, I spent so much time figuring out what to ask.

I found myself rewriting the same prompts over and over again, just trying to get the answers I wanted. Yet even after all that back and forth, the answers were still not as good as I expected.

I heard everyone talking about AI prompting. Some people were sharing powerful prompts online. Others were talking about how AI had transformed almost every aspect of their business.

Then I realised something.

It's great to want to use AI. It's even better to know how to give it stronger instructions.

Rather than wasting time going back and forth with AI, I wanted to understand how to give it stronger instructions and get better results.

So I started studying AI and prompting more deeply. I wanted to discover the real power of AI and understand what actually worked.

I started doing massive research, testing and comparison. I tried different prompts. I compared the results. I kept testing what worked and what didn't.

And eventually, I started saving the prompts that worked.

I started building a personal library.

That library eventually became The ChatGPT Prompt Encyclopedia — a collection of 500 battle-tested prompts designed around real business needs.

But that collection kept growing...

Until it became something much bigger.
